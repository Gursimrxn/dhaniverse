House pool: daily budgets

Each bucket may pay out at most 4% of its balance a day, measured at the first payout of each UTC day, so it can never run dry. Fixed rewards are funded by food revenue; surprises only by sponsor fees and yield; the safety bucket pays rescue meals only.

Float, buffer and yield

Sponsor money is held by FloatManager. A cash buffer covers three days of the most the live campaigns could pay; the rest earns yield in a venue you can withdraw from instantly. The yield goes to the house surprise bucket; sponsors' principal is always owed first.

Treasury split

The farm's harvest sales and ration-shop sales land in the Treasury; anyone can call split() to send its balance on: 60% house fixed, 10% house safety, 20% operations (gas, servers), 10% team.

Weekly draw

Tickets are earned free (streaks, Rush runs, quests), never bought. The prize comes from the house surprise bucket.
