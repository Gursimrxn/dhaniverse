# Player Progression

The onboarding sequence below is a suggested introduction, not a required
world-action order. [Choice design](14_Player_Choices_And_Consequences.md) lets
players change priorities and pursue any currently eligible opportunity.
Milestones describe possible progress; they do not force a single career.

## Onboarding Flow (First 30 Minutes)

### Phase 1: Identity & Basics (2 min)
- Create username and profile
- Receive starting resources: 100 coins, Tier 1 housing (3 days free), basic clothing
- Learn: Housing and clothing are requirements, not cosmetics

### Phase 2: Core Systems (5 min)
- Energy tutorial: Walk, see consumption, eat food, see recovery
- Health tutorial: See decay rate, understand consequences
- Stress tutorial: See meter, learn reduction through rest
- Learn: Three resources are interdependent

### Phase 3: First Work (10 min)
- Visit Labor Office, view available jobs
- Accept first job (Street Stall or Farm)
- Complete accelerated first shift (5 min tutorial time)
- Receive first payment, see Financial Dashboard
- Learn: Work = income. Different jobs pay differently.

### Phase 4: Needs Management (8 min)
- See the future rent deadline after the three free days; no charge is due during onboarding
- Visit market, purchase food
- Experience price fluctuation
- Return home, rest
- Learn: Expenses recur. Prices change. Budgeting is necessary.

### Phase 5: Goal Setting (5 min)
- Financial Dashboard review
- Offer authored guidance and let the player choose a goal such as an essentials buffer or literacy
- Goals are editable preferences, not permission gates; display Financial Health Score only once its normalization and empty-history rules are resolved
- Learn: Planning gives the player a reason for their next decision

## Progression Milestones

Day labels are illustrative pacing examples, not time locks, expected outcomes
for every player or requirements to follow this order. Actual gates belong to
the system owners. After these finite milestones, use [repeatable play](16_Lore_And_Repeatable_Play.md)
for activity mastery, personal projects and relationships in the changing town.

### Early Game (Days 1-7): Survival

| Day | Milestone | Requirements | Unlocks |
|-----|-----------|--------------|---------|
| 3 | Stable Housing | 10 shifts, rent paid, health >20%, stress <8 | Home Storage (Tier 2+) |
| 5 | Basic Literacy | Visit Library, 10 min course | Factory & Shop jobs |
| 7 | First Savings | Open bank account, deposit 50 coins | Interest accrual; loan-eligibility clock starts (eligibility itself per Banking S2) |

**Player State at Day 7:**
- Stable housing, consistent income
- Basic education, access to mid-tier jobs
- Bank account with small savings
- Financial Health Score: 30-40

---

### Mid Game (Days 8-30): Stability

| Day | Milestone | Requirements | Unlocks |
|-----|-----------|--------------|---------|
| 14 | School Diploma | 200 coins, 3 sessions (30 min) | Office entry-level jobs |
| 18 | Insurance Coverage | Visit Insurance Office, Basic Plan | 40% hospital coverage |
| 25 | Financial Health 60+ | Savings >10%, debt/income <30% | Business-track advisor guidance (hard gate is the Business prereq table) |

**Player State at Day 30:**
- Office-level employment
- Insurance protection
- Established reputation (Tier 3)
- Financial Health Score: 50-65
- Eligible for business ownership

---

### Late Game (Days 31-90): Growth

| Day | Milestone | Requirements | Unlocks |
|-----|-----------|--------------|---------|
| 40 | Business Ownership | Rep 41+, Diploma, Permit (150 coins), capital | Revenue from actual purchases while staffed; eligible employee hiring |
| 50 | College Degree | 800 coins, 6 sessions (60 min) | Manager/Senior jobs, specializations |
| 70 | Property Ownership | Down payment (20-50%), mortgage qualification | No rent, rental income, asset |
| 90 | Financial Health 80+ | Savings >20%, debt/income <15%, diverse expenses | Max loan ceiling, exclusive events |

**Player State at Day 90:**
- Property owner or business owner
- Advanced education
- Distinguished reputation (Tier 5)
- Financial Health Score: 75-85
- Multiple income streams

## Player Pathways

### The Fast Track (Aggressive)
**Style:** Risk-tolerant, time-intensive, growth-focused
**Strategy:**
- Maximize work shifts, minimal rest
- Informal loans for quick capital
- Delay insurance (risk)
- Rush education
- Aggressive business expansion

**Risks:** High stress, frequent illness, debt spiral, reputation damage
**Reward:** Fastest wealth accumulation, early business ownership

### The Steady Track (Balanced)
**Style:** Risk-averse, methodical, stability-focused
**Strategy:**
- Maintain health and stress carefully
- Save before spending
- Early insurance adoption
- Paced education
- Conservative business growth

**Risks:** Slower progression, missed opportunities
**Reward:** Stable growth, crisis resilience, strong reputation

### The Social Track (Community)
**Style:** Collaborative, relationship-oriented, trust-building
**Strategy:**
- Early SHG group formation
- Reputation building focus
- Player lending and trading
- Community challenges
- Social dining for reputation

**Risks:** Dependent on other players, group liability
**Reward:** Social safety net, better terms, community support

## Reasons to return

Players can return to improve at a favorite activity, finish a chosen home or
business project, meet familiar people, compare changed offers or participate
in an event. [Lore and repeatable play](16_Lore_And_Repeatable_Play.md) proposes
how these reasons continue after finite qualification and ownership milestones.
Enjoyable repetition, mastery and visible progress need actual playtests.

Rent, premiums, taxes, welfare reviews and other obligations retain their
owner-defined clocks. They are commitments to manage, not evidence that the game
is enjoyable or reasons to require compulsory daily attendance. Do not group a
30-day filing requirement under a weekly reset or infer a new cadence here.

## Failure & Recovery

### Bankruptcy Recovery
**Trigger:** 0 coins + eviction
**Response:**
1. Automatic Tier 0 housing (street)
2. Welfare office access unlocked
3. Basic jobs restored (Street Stall, Farm)
4. Debt forgiveness option (reputation penalty)
5. 7-day recovery period with reduced penalties

**Design Principle:** Always a path back. Failure teaches; it doesn't end the game.
