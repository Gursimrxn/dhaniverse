# Dhaniverse first neighborhood interaction briefs

**5 November 2026. Proposed interaction design for the accepted choice direction.**

These briefs make [player choices and consequences](14_Player_Choices_And_Consequences.md) concrete enough to build and review. They describe player interactions, authored objects, authoritative transitions and alternatives. They do not claim the services exist or introduce new pay, course, reputation or recovery rules. [Canonical rules](11_Canonical_Game_Rules.md) and system owners retain those decisions.

## Arriving without receiving a compulsory itinerary

The arrival desk provides the documented starter grant, basic clothes and three-day room grant once. The player sees the actual room address, a clear route to its bed, and three nearby opportunities: work, food and the library. The future rent notification describes the end of the free period; it does not charge on arrival.

Offer a suggested introduction with an explicit option to explore. Opening the job board, library or food counter does not require completion of a prior hint. Personal goals such as building a buffer or learning literacy are editable preferences. A skipped tutorial step never grants its economic outcome.

Registered identity still gates banking. Resolve whether arrival performs the free municipal registration through the proposed registry alias, or whether a real city-hall visit is required. Either route must be explained and navigable. Allocate a real available room atomically; when the finite rooms are full, use the defined capacity policy rather than silently duplicating a player's home.

## Market work

The activity episodes below are an initial minigame proposal, not the limit of
work gameplay. Review them for satisfying interaction, learnable skill, variety
and reasons to repeat. Existing attendance-based wages remain until an explicit
rule decision connects performance to rewards; optional status in this first
experiment does not make enjoyable work activities optional to the product.

**Intent:** earn money while deciding whether this workplace and its commitments suit the player. The job board and employer counter show qualification, current eligibility, duration, quoted pay calculation and interruption behavior before acceptance.

**Interaction:** the player enters a clear workplace boundary and accepts a shift. Inside, short optional episodes let them select produce for an order, arrange a usable counter or inspect a changed stock notice. Clear object feedback makes handovers satisfying. Employer goods remain visibly distinct from owned inventory. Ambient animation and player purchases must be distinguishable in economic records.

**Choice and connection:** take the available role, visit another eligible workplace, study first or postpone work. During the shift, ordinary conversation and viewing a financial summary can be allowed; study, treatment and another shift cannot overlap where the canonical compatibility rules prohibit them. Leaving early settles the documented partial pay and changes what the player has time and cash to do next.

**Objects and authority:** job board, quote surface, counter, employer trays, serving point, receiving niche and a readable workplace boundary. A server-owned shift records trusted presence, accepted terms, time and settlement. Purchase transactions, optional visual tasks and wages are separate. Reconnection cannot pay the same shift again.

**Open decisions:** attendance evidence and settlement boundaries, the exact compatible viewing interactions, and what makes a normal 30-minute shift enjoyable. Preserve the five-minute tutorial exception and current wages while testing. Do not invent performance pay for completing the optional episodes.

**Review:** observe whether the player understands the commitment, voluntarily engages with the workplace, leaves intentionally and can explain their wages. Test interruption and retries separately from enjoyment.

## Food comparison and purchasing

**Intent:** recover enough for a chosen activity while keeping useful money available. The market counter presents a small catalog with current quotes, actual stock, quantities and documented effects. Common Table provides its own appropriate food/refuge role; it must not become a premium cafe menu merely because of its facade.

**Interaction:** the player compares foods, selects a quantity and accepts the shown offer. The item enters owned inventory after commitment. Consuming is a separate action with visible condition changes. Keeping an item for later remains possible, subject to expiry and capacity. A decorative produce crate is never an inventory source.

**Choice and connection:** buy the minimum needed now, select different effects, keep more cash or compare another seller. The result changes reachable work, study or recovery options. These outcomes come from real effects and rules, not a mandatory 'eat now' quest.

**Objects and authority:** visible stocked counter, price surface, goods display and inventory/consumption feedback. Purchase commits money and stock atomically. Consumption removes the owned quantity and applies its effects once. A changed price or unavailable item requires a refreshed offer and new consent, with no partial charge.

**Open decisions:** which narrow food set provides genuine alternatives at canonical prices, per-item baseline stock and price-engine inputs, and a fully feasible zero-cash food/recovery path. Check whether one item dominates the others; do not adjust its price in a tutorial script.

**Review:** players can explain their selection and what they retained. Two buyers cannot both receive the same last item. Declining a refreshed quote leaves resources unchanged.

## Library learning

**Intent:** improve future opportunities or obtain the documented learning benefits instead of maximizing immediate wages. The library is visible from a familiar route and shows the free literacy course's actual ten-minute commitment and qualification outcome.

**Interaction:** the player can inspect courses and eligible job outcomes before enrolling. A proposed reading activity uses a short, readable comparison of offers or costs in the world. Its educational purpose is reasoning, not receiving coins for choosing the designer's preferred lifestyle. Keep the course's documented no-energy library exception; ordinary travel still follows its own rules.

**Choice and connection:** literacy before the first shift, literacy after saving a buffer, a different eligible course, or continued entry work. Completion must make at least one shop/workshop role genuinely available through its remaining gates. Reserve usable qualified-work premises and confirm its job/service mapping; a locked decorative workshop does not validate progression.

**Objects and authority:** entry, course notice, readable material, seating and a nearby understandable route to qualified work. Course records and qualification belong to the server. Existing completion/repeat and interruption rules apply; an optional reading exercise cannot independently award qualification or bypass the timer.

**Open decisions:** course interruption semantics, compatibility with rest and other activities, and whether the proposed activity improves understanding. Consult education's owner rather than copying work's disconnect rule. Assessment gates or altered awards require a separate rule decision.

**Review:** test library-first and wages-first players. Both can continue playing; the newly qualified player sees an actual additional choice. Neither must adopt a fixed career to continue.

## Saving and returning home

**Intent:** decide how much money is needed soon and what can be put aside. At the bank, show available cash, savings, relevant withdrawals and known upcoming obligations. Present transfers as transfers; depositing does not increase income or net worth.

**Interaction:** enter a chosen amount, inspect the result and confirm. Ordinary savings and fixed deposits must be clearly different products; the pilot can ship ordinary savings alone. Returning home brings the player to the same addressed room and bed, where rate-based rest shows actual progress and what can be done alongside it.

**Choice and connection:** keep cash for purchases, deposit some, withdraw accessible savings, rest or change a goal. Obligations do not disappear because a goal changes. Future education and housing plans can compete without either being a compulsory upgrade.

**Objects and authority:** practical bank counter/ATM, a private statement surface, room address, permissions and landlord-provided Tier 1 bed. Transfers preserve total money. Lease, rent, bed access and recovery depend on authoritative state, not a client-owned location menu.

**Open decisions:** rest versus passive drain, activity compatibility, the funding account for each obligation and deduction priority, and the route after eviction. A proposed UI 'buffer' is advice, not an escrow or exemption from expenses.

**Review:** work after withdrawal, purchase before deposit, change goals and reconnect. Verify the same address and committed balances. Measure the real recovery duration instead of using an instant recharge for the demonstration.

## First player trade

**Intent:** obtain a needed owned item or sell surplus through a voluntary deal. For the first full-game interaction prototype, prefer the documented face-to-face direct trade; asynchronous listings can follow with their distinct fee and escrow rules. This selection does not redefine a separate event submission's scope.

**Interaction:** one player initiates; both inspect the complete offer, quantities and ownership. Confirmation applies to the same offer version. Changing the offer invalidates confirmations before exchange. Explain the finality of committed trades and any applicable reputation consequence before acceptance.

**Choice and connection:** compare the trade with another offer, keep the item, go to a market or decline. A completed trade changes real inventory and money and may establish a familiar counterparty. Declining before agreement must be distinguished from the documented cancellation-after-agreement rule; define precisely when that rule applies before shipping.

**Objects and authority:** a usable shared trade space, nearby-player interaction and a clear confirmation surface. Validate identity, presence, owned transferable items, capacity, funds and both parties' consent. Transfer both sides atomically and retain one durable receipt per committed exchange. Active business stock and protected documents are not transferable through ordinary player trade.

**Open decisions:** disconnect/cancellation lifecycle, bounded offer cleanup and collusive reputation farming. The reference's repeatable reputation award creates a potential circular-trade exploit; resolve the policy explicitly rather than enabling a new progression shortcut or silently changing the reward.

**Review:** simultaneous acceptance, offer edits, one player walking away, disconnect during confirmation, retries and rejected capacity. An empty-world player still has access to independently usable work, food and recovery.

## Review different first sessions

Use identical starter grants to compare three candidate experiences. These are illustrative possibilities, not guaranteed payouts or scripted outcomes.

| Player intention | Possible order | Meaningful next goal |
|---|---|---|
| Feel financially secure | Inspect the room and future rent, work, compare food, retain an essentials buffer, rest | Understand an attainable daily budget and choose whether to study next |
| Improve opportunities | Find the library, complete literacy under its rules, inspect a real qualified role, buy food when useful, work | Decide whether the additional work makes paid school worth saving toward |
| Meet people and try trading | Explore shared routes, compare a real player offer with the market, accept or decline, do eligible work, return home | Recognize a counterparty, choose a possible future deal or pursue a different priority |

A shared-stock change should give each player a reason to reconsider something, without writing an identical compulsory replacement task. Review whether they can explain that change and their response. Then run the same services with nobody else online and in the hardship states listed in [the choice chapter](14_Player_Choices_And_Consequences.md#consequences-with-a-way-forward).

Keep the scope narrow enough to verify: one initial workplace, one genuinely unlocked additional role, a small food catalog, ordinary savings, addressed lease/rest, essential recovery, literacy and direct trade. Complete systems and finished art are separate claims. The first result should already permit different orders and priorities.
