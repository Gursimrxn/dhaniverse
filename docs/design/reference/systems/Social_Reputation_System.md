Social Reputation represents the player's **standing in the community** — how they are perceived by employers, institutions, vendors, and other players in Dhaniverse.  
It is an invisible but consequential layer that opens doors, improves terms, and reflects the cumulative effect of lifestyle, appearance, and behavior over time.

Social Reputation interacts with:
• [[Work System]]  
• [[Commerce_System]]  
• [[Banking_Finance_System]]  
• [[Government_Legal_System]]  
• [[Restaurant_Dhaba_System]]  
• [[Education_System]]

---

## Reputation Scale

Social Reputation is measured on a scale of **0 to 100**.  
It is divided into five tiers:

| Tier | Range  | Label         | General Effect                            |
|------|--------|---------------|-------------------------------------------|
| 1    | 0–20   | Unknown       | No advantages; some doors closed          |
| 2    | 21–40  | Recognized    | Minor trust advantages; basic access      |
| 3    | 41–60  | Established   | Better job terms; improved loan rates     |
| 4    | 61–80  | Respected     | Preferred access; discounts; social events|
| 5    | 81–100 | Distinguished | Maximum benefits; exclusive opportunities |

## Reputation Systems

#### Gaining Reputation

**S1 — Work Consistency**  
Completing job shifts without absences or early exits gains reputation.  
Each **completed shift** adds **+0.5 Rep points**.  
Completing **10 consecutive shifts without a miss** adds a bonus **+3 Rep points**.

---

**S2 — Education Completion**  
Completing each education level adds reputation:

| Education Level     | Rep Gained |
|---------------------|------------|
| Basic Literacy      | +2         |
| School Diploma      | +5         |
| College Degree      | +10        |
| Specialization      | +5 each    |

---

**S3 — Dining and Social Activities**  
Business dining at Restaurants grants reputation as defined in [[Restaurant & Dhaba System]].  
Higher-tier establishments grant more:

| Establishment    | Rep Gained per Visit |
|------------------|----------------------|
| Dhaba            | 0                    |
| Café             | +0.5                 |
| Restaurant       | +1                   |
| Fine Dining      | +2 to +5             |

---

**S4 — Financial Reliability**  
Meeting financial obligations on time builds trust:

| Action                          | Rep Gained |
|---------------------------------|------------|
| Rent paid on time               | +0.5       |
| Loan repayment on time          | +0.5       |
| Tax filed on time               | +1         |
| Insurance premium paid on time  | +0.5       |

---

**S5 — Community Contributions**  
Certain game events allow players to contribute to their community.  
Examples: donating to welfare events, helping SHG members, participating in civic activities.  
Each contribution adds **+1 to +3 Rep points** depending on the action.

---

#### Losing Reputation

**S6 — Financial Defaults**  
Missing financial obligations damages reputation:

| Action                          | Rep Lost |
|---------------------------------|----------|
| Rent unpaid (eviction)          | −5       |
| Loan defaulted                  | −8       |
| Tax fine issued                 | −3       |
| Medical bill unpaid             | −2       |
| Insurance claim fraud (if added)| −15      |

---

**S7 — Legal Events**  
Police detention, court losses, and permit violations reduce reputation:

| Event                          | Rep Lost |
|--------------------------------|----------|
| Detained by police             | −5       |
| Lost court case                | −3       |
| Operating without permit       | −4       |
| Tax audit failure              | −6       |

---

**S8 — Appearance and Lifestyle**  
Worn-out clothing visible in public reduces reputation slowly:

| Clothing State                  | Rep Effect                     |
|---------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Clothing at full durability     | No effect                      |
| Clothing worn out (degraded)    | −0.5 Rep per in-game day       |
| No clothing (Tier 0)            | −1 Rep per in-game day         |

---

**S9 — Illness in Public**
Being visibly Ill (see [[Illness_State]]) while in a social or work setting causes reputation loss:  
−2 Rep per in-game day while Ill and outside the home.

## Reputation Effects by Tier

#### Tier 1 — Unknown (0–20)

- No access to high-tier restaurant seating
- Bank loan interest rate is at maximum
- Some office jobs require a minimum reputation of 21 to apply
- Informal lender may charge higher rates

---

#### Tier 2 — Recognized (21–40)

- Standard access to most services
- No special advantages
- Microfinance SHG membership is easier to obtain

---

#### Tier 3 — Established (41–60)

- Bank loan interest rate reduced by **1%**
- Office job applications receive **priority consideration**
- Property broker provides **one free valuation per 10 in-game days**
- Restaurant reservation wait time reduced

---

#### Tier 4 — Respected (61–80)

- Bank loan interest rate reduced by **2%**
- Work System: **+10% pay** at office-level jobs (employer trust premium)
- Clothing shop offers a **10% loyalty discount**
- Court appeal success rate increases by **+10%**
- Welfare office processes benefits faster (no wait period)

---

#### Tier 5 — Distinguished (81–100)

- Bank loan interest rate reduced by **3%**; higher loan ceiling unlocked
- **+20% pay** at senior office and manager jobs
- Fine dining establishments offer access to **exclusive events** (game events unlocking investment or business opportunities)
- Police station interactions are handled with reduced detention risk
- Tax office grants a **7-day extension** on filing deadlines without penalty

---

## Reputation Decay

**S10 — Passive Decay**  
Reputation does not stay static without effort.  
If the player takes **no reputation-gaining actions for 5 in-game days**, reputation decays at **−1 point per day** until they resume active play.  
This prevents reputation from being permanently "banked" early and ignored.

---

**S11 — Reputation Floor**  
Reputation cannot fall below **0**.  
At 0, the player is at maximum disadvantage but not permanently locked out of recovery.

---

## Why Social Reputation Exists

Reputation is the **long-term consequence layer** for how the player has lived — not just financially, but socially.  
It rewards consistency, education, and financial reliability while punishing recklessness, default, and neglect.  
It also means that **two players with identical coin balances can have very different access to opportunities** depending on how they earned and spent those coins.  
This reflects a real-world truth: wealth and reputation are related but not the same thing.
