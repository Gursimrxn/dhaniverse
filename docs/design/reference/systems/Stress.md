Stress represents the player’s **mental pressure and emotional state** in Dhaniverse.  
It reflects how well the player is managing responsibilities such as **bills, lifestyle choices, and financial stability**.

Stress fluctuates depending on situations the player experiences in the world.

---

#### How Stress Appears in the Game

Stress is displayed as a **Stress Meter** ranging from **1 to 12**.
As stress increases, the player experiences **negative effects on health and energy consumption**.
When stress is low, the player’s **[[Health]] can recover more efficiently**.

## Stress Systems

#### Stress Levels

**S1 — Low Stress**

If the **Stress Meter is between 1–4**, the player is considered to be in **Low Stress**.
Low stress allows the **Health system to recover** according to the health recovery rules.

---

**S2 — Moderate Stress**

If the **Stress Meter is between 5–8**, the player is considered to be in **Moderate Stress**.
Moderate stress causes **slow health reduction over time**.

---

**S3 — High Stress**

If the **Stress Meter is between 9–12**, the player is considered to be in **High Stress**.
High stress causes:
- **Faster health decay**
- **[[Energy]] consumption to increase**
---

#### Lifestyle Influence on Stress
Player lifestyle choices can change stress levels.

---

**S4 — Fast Food Lifestyle**

If a player continuously consumes **FAST FOOD**, stress increases.
"Constantly (xN)" is defined canonically as: **N qualifying items consumed
within one 24 real-hour window** (the counter resets each day; items are
any Fast-category food per [Food.md](Food.md)).
- Low Stress + fast food x10 in one day → Stress becomes **Moderate** (set to 5)
- Moderate Stress + fast food x7 in one day → Stress becomes **High** (set to 9)
- High Stress + fast food x5 in one day → Player becomes **Ill** (subject to
  the 10-in-game-day Illness frequency cap in [[Illness_State]])

---

**S5 — Natural Food Lifestyle**

If a player continuously consumes **NATURAL FOOD**, stress decreases.
Same daily-window counting as S4:
- Moderate Stress + natural food x7 in one day → Stress becomes **Low** (set to 4)
- High Stress + natural food x5 in one day → Stress becomes **Moderate** (set to 8)

---

#### Stress Triggers
Stress levels can also change due to **life situations** experienced by the player.
Examples include:

- Bills not paid on time → Stress increases
- Financial pressure → Stress increases
- Having insurance coverage → Stress decreases
- Maintaining financial stability → Stress decreases
    
---

## Why Stress Is Important

Stress represents the **mental burden of managing life responsibilities**.
It ensures that player behavior such as **financial planning, food choices, and lifestyle habits** affects their physical condition and efficiency.
Higher stress makes survival harder by accelerating **health decay and energy consumption**.
