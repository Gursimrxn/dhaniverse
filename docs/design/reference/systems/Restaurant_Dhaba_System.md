Restaurants and Dhabas are **sit-down dining establishments** in Dhaniverse.  
They are distinct from street stalls and food vendors — they cost more, take time, but provide benefits beyond raw energy recovery that no other food source offers.

Restaurants and Dhabas interact with:
• [[Food]]  
• [[Energy]]  
• [[Health]]  
• [[Stress]]  
• [[Social Reputation System]]  
• Economy

---

| Type         | Price Level | Atmosphere    | Primary Benefit         |
|--------------|-------------|---------------|-------------------------|
| Dhaba        | Low         | Casual, local | Cheap full meals + mild stress relief |
| Café         | Medium      | Relaxed       | Stress relief + social rep gain     |
| Restaurant   | High        | Formal        | Full energy + health boost + social rep |
| Fine Dining  | Very High   | Premium       | Maximum benefits + major social rep |

---

## How Restaurants Differ from Food Vendors

Food purchased from **street stalls or markets** provides:
- Energy recovery only (as per [[Food]])
- Instant consumption, no time cost

Food consumed at a **Restaurant or Dhaba** provides:
- Energy recovery (same as food item)
- **Stress reduction** (unique to sit-down dining)
- **Social reputation gain** (unique to restaurants and above)
- A **Rest Bonus** applied to the next energy bar

The trade-off is **time and cost**.

## Dining Rules

**S1 — Time Cost**  
Dining at a restaurant takes **real-world time**. The player must remain seated for the duration.

| Establishment | Dining Duration   |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Dhaba         | 3 minutes         |
| Café          | 5 minutes         |
| Restaurant    | 8 minutes         |
| Fine Dining   | 12 minutes        |

During dining, the player **cannot move or perform tasks**.  
The idle energy recovery timer (see [[Energy System]] Rule S8) applies during dining — the player may recover 5% energy if they dine for the full idle window.

---

**S2 — Rest Bonus**  
After completing a full meal at any establishment, the player's **next energy bar lasts 20% longer** than normal.  
This reflects the sustained energy from a proper meal as opposed to eating on the go.

---

**S3 — Stress Relief Cap**  
Stress relief from dining cannot reduce Stress below **Level 1 (minimum)**.  
Dining stress relief cannot be stacked — the player must wait **2 in-game hours** between stress-relieving meals.

---

**S4 — Health Recovery from Dining**  
Health recovery from restaurant meals applies only if the meal is **Natural Food category**.  
Dhaba thali, restaurant main course, and fine dining set menu qualify.  
Café sandwiches and fast-food-style café items do not provide health recovery.

---

**S5 — Business Dining and Social Rep**  
The **Business Lunch** and **Client Dinner** options are designed for social interaction.  
These meals grant elevated **Social Reputation** gains (see [[Social_Reputation_System]]).  
They can be used to improve job prospects or unlock certain social events in the game.

---

**S6 — Dining and Clothing**  
Restaurants and Fine Dining have **dress code requirements**.

| Establishment | Minimum Clothing Tier Required |
|---------------|-------------------------------|
| Dhaba         | None                          |
| Café          | Tier 1 (Basic clothes)        |
| Restaurant    | Tier 2 (Smart casuals)        |
| Fine Dining   | Tier 3 (Formal wear)          |

Players who arrive below the minimum tier are **refused entry**.  
Reference: [[Commerce System]] — Clothing Tiers.

---

**S7 — Dhaba as Stress Refuge**  
The Dhaba is the only food location accessible to players in **High Stress or Illness state** that provides any stress reduction.  
This ensures low-income players always have access to at least minimal stress relief without expensive options.

---

## Why Restaurants Exist as a Separate System

Street food solves energy. Restaurants solve stress.  
This distinction is intentional — it forces the player to evaluate dining as an **investment in mental health**, not just fuel.  
A player who only eats cheap street food will maintain energy but accumulate stress over time.  
A player who dines out regularly spends more coins but keeps stress low, which keeps health stable, which keeps work productivity high.  
The Dhaba bridges affordability and benefit — it is the realistic solution for players who cannot afford restaurants but need more than stall food provides.
