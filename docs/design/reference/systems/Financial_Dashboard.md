The Financial Dashboard is the **explicit teaching layer** of Dhaniverse.
It surfaces the numbers behind the player's financial life — income, expenses, savings rate, net worth, debt — in clear, readable form.

Without the dashboard, players feel the consequences of their decisions but may not understand why. The dashboard closes that gap. It is where personal finance education becomes explicit.

It interacts with:
• All economic systems
• [[Work System]]
• [[Banking & Finance System]]
• [[Business Ownership System]]
• [[Housing System]]
• [[Healthcare System]]
• [[Insurance System]]

## Dashboard Sections

The Financial Dashboard has five panels, accessible from any screen via the player menu.

---

### Panel 1 — Daily Summary

Shows the player's financial activity for the current in-game day.

| Field                | Description                                      |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Income Today         | Total coins earned (all sources)                 |
| Expenses Today       | Total coins spent (all categories)               |
| Net Today            | Income minus Expenses                            |
| Savings Rate         | (Income − Expenses) ÷ Income × 100 shown as %   |

Savings Rate is highlighted in:
- Green if above 20%
- Yellow if 10–20%
- Red if below 10% or negative

This is the single most important number on the dashboard — players learn to track it.

---

### Panel 2 — Expense Breakdown

Shows where coins went, broken down by category.

| Category         | Example Expenses                              |
|------------------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Housing          | Rent, mortgage installment                    |
| Food             | All food purchases                            |
| Healthcare       | Treatment, medicine, insurance premium        |
| Transport        | Bus tickets, train fares, telecom plan        |
| Education        | School/college fees, student loan repayment   |
| Debt Repayment   | Bank loan, microfinance, player loan          |
| Business Costs   | Stock, rent, wages, tax (if business owner)   |
| Other            | Clothing, electronics, miscellaneous          |

A visual bar shows proportion of income consumed by each category. Players who spend more than 40% on any single category receive a gentle flag: "Housing is taking up a large portion of your income."

---

### Panel 3 — Net Worth Tracker

Net Worth = All Assets − All Liabilities

| Assets                  | Liabilities              |
|-------------------------|--------------------------|
| Wallet (carried coins)  | Bank loan balance        |
| Bank account balance    | Microfinance loan balance|
| Fixed deposit value     | Player loan balance      |
| Investment portfolio    | Informal lender balance  |
| Property value          | Pawn shop pledge         |
| Business estimated value| Unpaid bills             |
| Inventory item value    | Unpaid rent (if any)     |

Net Worth is displayed as a **7-day line graph** so players can visually see if they are trending up or down.

A positive slope means wealth is being built. A flat line means the player is treading water. A downward slope triggers an advisory: "Your net worth has declined for 3 days. Review your expense breakdown."

---

### Panel 4 — Debt Monitor

A dedicated debt panel showing all active liabilities in one place.

| Field              | Shown For Each Debt                          |
|--------------------|----------------------------------------------|
| Lender             | Bank / Microfinance / Player Name / Informal |
| Original Amount    | Total borrowed                               |
| Remaining Balance  | How much is left                             |
| Interest Rate      | Annual/flat rate                             |
| Next Payment Due   | In-game hours until next deduction           |
| Total Interest Paid| How much extra has been paid beyond principal|

Total Interest Paid is the most educational field here. Seeing the cumulative cost of borrowing makes the concept of interest visceral rather than abstract.

A "Cost of Debt" summary at the bottom shows: "You have paid X coins in interest across all loans."

---

### Panel 5 — Financial Health Score

A single composite score from 0 to 100 that summarises the player's overall financial condition.

| Component              | Weight | What It Measures                        |
|------------------------|--------|-----------------------------------------|
| Savings Rate           | 25%    | Surplus after expenses                  |
| Debt-to-Income Ratio   | 25%    | Monthly debt payments vs. income        |
| Net Worth Trend        | 20%    | Direction of wealth over last 7 days    |
| Expense Diversity      | 15%    | No single category dominating spend     |
| Emergency Buffer       | 15%    | Bank balance ≥ 3 days of expenses       |

Score interpretation:

| Score  | Label              | Advisory                                          |
|--------|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| 80–100 | Financially Stable | No action needed                                  |
| 60–79  | Managing           | One area needs attention — shown specifically     |
| 40–59  | At Risk            | Spending exceeds income or debt is high           |
| 20–39  | Struggling         | Multiple systems under stress                     |
| 0–19   | Crisis             | Immediate action required — specific steps shown  |

When the score drops below 40, the dashboard surfaces a **3-step action list** tailored to the player's specific situation. Example: "1. Reduce food spend by switching to market purchases. 2. Defer clothing purchase until rent is secured. 3. Consider microfinance group loan instead of informal lender."

## Financial Advisor

The Financial Advisor is a **character** attached to the Dashboard who comments on player decisions in plain language.

The Advisor speaks at three moments:

**On first occurrence of a new financial event:**
- First loan taken: "You borrowed X coins at Y% interest. This means you will repay X + interest. Make sure your income covers the monthly payment."
- First insurance claim: "Your insurance covered X coins of your hospital bill. Without it, you would have paid X coins more. That is what insurance is for."
- First investment loss: "Market prices fell and your portfolio lost value. This is normal — markets move up and down. The question is whether you invested more than you can afford to lose."

**On poor decisions detected:**
- High Stress + fast food habit: "You have eaten fast food 8 times today. This is increasing your stress and reducing your health, which will slow your energy recovery and reduce your work earnings."
- Missed loan payment: "You missed a repayment. This adds a late fee and damages your reputation. Set a reminder or keep a buffer in your bank account."

**On good decisions:**
- Savings rate above 30% for 3 days: "Your savings rate is strong. You are spending less than you earn consistently. This is the foundation of financial stability."
- Paying off a loan fully: "Loan cleared. You are no longer paying interest on this debt. That coins stays in your pocket from now on."

The Advisor never lectures. One sentence per trigger. Plain language, specific numbers.

## Multiplayer Comparison (Optional View)

Players can opt into a **comparison view** that shows their Financial Health Score alongside anonymised peers at the same in-game age (days played).

Shows:
- Their score vs. average score of peers
- Their net worth vs. peer average
- Savings rate vs. peer average

This is opt-in and shows no individual player's name — only aggregated peer data. It creates **social motivation** without public shaming.

## Why the Financial Dashboard Exists

Every other system in Dhaniverse teaches through consequence — you get sick, you lose income, you pay interest. The Dashboard teaches through **reflection** — you see the numbers, understand the pattern, and make a better decision next time.

The savings rate, debt monitor, and Financial Health Score are real tools used in real personal finance. Players who use them in the game will recognise them when they encounter them in real life — and know what they mean.
