# Where the assets come from

## Audio (`game/assets/audio/`)

Every sound here is original: generated by `tools/make_audio.js` from noise and sine waves, with no samples and no third-party recordings. Nothing to license.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `coin`, `bell`, `paper`, `door`, `ferry_horn` | Things that happen |
| `step_stone_*`, `step_grass_*`, `step_wood_*` | Footsteps by surface |
| `amb_water`, `amb_town`, `amb_market`, `amb_table`, `amb_bank` | A loop for each kind of place |
| `music_dawn`, `music_day`, `music_night` | Original plucked lines over a drone, in a major pentatonic |

## Motion

The way people move (walking, running, starting, stopping, turning, reaching, sitting) is original: worked out at runtime by `game/scripts/valley/gait.gd` from each body's own skeleton. No motion library. The idle underneath is the character pack's.

## Characters and foliage

See the headers of `game/scripts/life/person.gd` (Quaternius Ultimate Animated Character Pack, CC0).
